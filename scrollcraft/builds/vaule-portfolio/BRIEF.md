   drawing set. A real title block (Schriftfeld) follows you and *is* the

   exploded under your scroll.

7. **World vs scenes:** distinct sheets of one drawing set (scenes), not one
   continuous flight.
8. **Assets:** none supplied, no KIE_AI_API_KEY set. So: no generated
   photography. The world is drawn: SVG line work, real markup, zero raster
   imagery. This is the honest choice, not a fallback: a stock-looking photo of
   "a developer" would weaken it.

## Grammar: Drawing set (new, defined here)

- **Unit:** the sheet (Blatt). Each project is a sheet with its own drawing.
- **Chrome:** a fixed drawing frame around the viewport and a title block in
  the bottom-right corner. The title block shows the current sheet's name,
  field and number, and opens the sheet index (jumps). It also holds DE/EN.
- **Sequence:** cover sheet → project sheets → parts list → release.
- **Ending:** the release (Freigabe): the title block, enlarged to full size,
  carrying the links. The CTA is a field in it, not a button island.
- **Bans:** photography and scrims, kinetic headline stacks, spotlight, magnet,
  centred copy, feature cards, scroll cues, drift as gradient (grounds are
  either film or cyanotype, hard edge).
- Sheet numbers are allowed because in a drawing set they are information
  (the index jumps by them). Callout numbers 1–3 on the exploded view and Pos.
  numbers in the parts list are real references, not decoration.

## Revision 2 (2026-11-25): user feedback

User: liked the site and the graphics, but "way too much in that CAD / drawing
style", wants more colour, and a dark/light toggle "with a really really fancy
animation".

What changed:
- Removed every piece of drawing chrome: frame, title block, sheet numbers,
  drafting grid, lettering guides, parts-list table, release stamp, blueprint
  grid, section letters. The graphics stay, recoloured.
- Grammar is now **colour chapters**: each project owns a hue (Apollo red,
  Vantage pink, Daybreak orange, Copperplate patina green, WalkOff green, ECU
  yellow, TrackPegs cobalt); its chapter ground is tinted with it and its
  headline and graphic carry it. Hard ground cuts between chapters.
- Nav: floating dock (current chapter + its colour dot, contents popover,
  DE/EN, theme toggle).
- Hero: Tyrolean ridges as five planes (sky + sun/moon, far, mid, name, near).
  The near ridge overtakes the name and becomes the page.
- Signature move: the theme switch is a nightfall/daybreak. The new theme is
  revealed behind a mountain-ridge clip rising from the bottom (View
  Transitions), with a twilight glow band riding its edge; in the hero the sun
  sets and the moon rises. Reduced motion or no View Transitions: instant swap.
- Peak unchanged in mechanism (TrackPegs section + explode), now on cobalt with
  the tracker lit yellow.

Feeling curve: unchanged. Tell-someone sentence now: "it's the site where
switching to dark mode makes night climb up over the mountains."
